Our Events

How we enrich the community

Oom Piet's Christmas Party

We’ve been hosting our flagship event in Durbanville for over 10 years. This annual event brings families together for a wondrous evening show featuring your child’s favourite friends, firefighters, magicians, and of course Santa.

Durbanville Duck Derby

The Duck Derby offers a thrilling day out for families to sponsor and support their duck as it competes against hundreds more. Racing down our watercourse at the scenic Bloemendal Trail Park, each duck affords an opportunity to win grand prizes!

Easter Egg Hunt

Our annual children’s Easter Egg Hunt allows children to explore and hunt for hidden Easter eggs with their friends; as well as take part in some friendly competitions with awesome prizes on offer.

Our Projects

How we use our funds

Commnunity Health Day

Our club hosts a Community Health Day once year in Klipheuwel and Fisantekraal, offering high-quality healthcare services to the local community. Medical students get an opportunity to gain practical experience and participate in outreach.

School maintenance

Rotary Club of Durbanville supports 9 under-resourced schools in the Northern Suburbs, providing essential maintenance and resources; ensuring the learners’ right to education is upheld.

Recognising academic brilliance

We host an annual academic prize-giving for our supported schools, recognizing learners’ achievements with a donation of items that supports their future education. This initiative promotes real ambition in our next generation.

Mandela Day

We honour the legacy of Madiba by orchestrating a large-scale food drive that provides balanced meals to over 3 500 empoverished school children.

Interact

The Rotary Club of Durbanville proudly sponsors the Interact Clubs of Fairmont High and Curro Durbanville High. Interactors are given opportunities to plan and execute on their own community improvement initiatives.
