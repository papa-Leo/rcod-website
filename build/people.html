Want to get involved? We have a wonderful group of volunteers who help to make the club’s vision come true; including Interactors, Inner Wheelers, family, friends, and neighbours. We welcome anyone willing to assist with our projects and make Durbanville a better place - no hard commitments! Click here for more info.

More? Becoming a Rotarian gives you the chance to develop skills like public speaking, project management, and event planning. You’ll meet interesting people from our district and around the world; and you’ll tackle local and international issues that are important to you and your fellow club members.
