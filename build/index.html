People of Action.

The Rotary Club of Durbanville is dedicated to uplifting its community through local collaboration. We partner with local businesses and organizations to bring value to the Durbanville community; and the funds we raise are used for projects that improve the Durbanville area.

Where you've seen us recently

Duck Derby

The Duck Derby offers a thrilling day out for families to sponsor and support their duck as it competes against hundreds more. Racing down our watercourse at the scenic Bloemendal Trail Park, each duck affords an opportunity to win grand prizes!

Community Health Day

Our club hosts a Community Health Day once year in Klipheuwel and Fisantekraal, offering high-quality healthcare services to the local community. Medical students get an opportunity to gain practical experience and participate in outreach.

School maintenance

Rotary Club of Durbanville supports 9 under-resourced schools in the Northern Suburbs, providing essential maintenance and resources; ensuring the learners’ right to education is upheld.
